Custom statuses

Add your own status beyond the built-in six — pick a color and a name. It becomes a full status: it shows up in every dropdown, gets its own quick-set button on the drawing, its own header count, and its own row in printed reports.

Equipment catalog

Upload a CSV of expected equipment tags (one tag per row; an optional second column can hold a description). Once loaded, typing in the equipment name field while placing a box will suggest matching tags — useful for keeping tag numbers consistent across 40+ drawings instead of retyping them by hand.
